# Introduction to Workshops

Welcome to the Workshops section! This area features hands-on workshops and interactive sessions designed for collaborative learning and skill-building.

## Workshop Format

Our workshops typically include:
- **Interactive Sessions**: Collaborative problem-solving and group activities
- **Hands-on Practice**: Real-world applications and exercises
- **Q&A Segments**: Open discussion and clarification opportunities
- **Resource Sharing**: Access to materials, tools, and references
