#### References

The primary reference for the first half of the course is the following book:

[Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach, 4th US ed.](https://aima.cs.berkeley.edu/)
by [Stuart Russell](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~russell/) and [Peter Norvig](https://www.norvig.com/)

You can find a copy of the PDF [here](https://people.engr.tamu.edu/guni/csce642/files/AI_Russell_Norvig.pdf). By and large, the following sections of the book are the most relevant:

1. Games: sections 5.1, 5.2, and 5.3
2. CSPs: sections 6.1, 6.2, and 6.3
3. MDPs: sections 17.1, 17.2, and 17.3

Some of the lectures in this [set of Youtube videos](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCDZUttQj8ytfASQIcvsLYgg) on CSPs and MDPs would be relevant.
