#### Grading Policy

1. **Quizzes**: 50% <br /> (6 quizzes, each worth 10%, best 5 considered)
    - The dates of the quizzes are enlisted in the schedule.
    - Quizzes will be open-notes and objective-type questions.
    - Access to internet and collaboration with peers is NOT allowed.
    - All quizzes are conducted in class.
2. **Exams**: 40% <br /> (2 exams, each worth 20%)
    - The exams will be held during the midsem and endsem weeks.
    - They syllabus for the midsem exam includes: games, CSPs, and MDPs.
    - The endsem exam will cover the topics covered in the second half of the course.
3. **Project**: 10% <br /> (1 project, details shared over Google Classroom)
