#### 2025 · Aug-Nov Term  

Find all notes from class [here](https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/l07s5t6d649wjjvb30miw/2025-03-ES214-class-notes.pdf?rlkey=56e5as3dw2umr8n4l3zo96zmd&st=dvotxebp&dl=0). Note: unedited and real-time, so expect these to be generally sloppy, illegible, and mostly useless, but hopefully also mostly harmless :)

| Date | Lecture |
|------|---------|
| 06 Oct, 2025 | _MidSem Review_ <br /> Explored some properties of kings in tournaments. <br /> See [this article](https://works.swarthmore.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1145&context=fac-math-stat) on the **The King Chicken Theorems** by Stephen B. Maurer for more. |
| 07 Oct, 2025 | _Birkhoff–von-Neumann Theorem_ <br /> See the [wikiedia entry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doubly_stochastic_matrix) on the topic for a proof. Slides from class.  |
| 10 Oct, 2025 | _Picture-Hanging Puzzles_ <br /> See [this paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1203.3602) for more context. |
| 13 Oct, 2025 | _100 Prisoners Puzzle_ <br /> See this [nice video by Veritasium](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iSNsgj1OCLA) and the [wikipedia page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/100_prisoners_problem). |
| 14 Oct, 2025 | _Linear Algebra with Oddtown_ <br /> See [the first part of this document](https://www.math.cmu.edu/users/math/math/jmackey/math301/oddtown.pdf) for the approach discussed in class, [Theorem 1.1 here](https://home.ttic.edu/~madhurt/courses/reu2013/class726.pdf) for a determinant-based argument for linear independence, and [Theorem 2 here](https://www.uvm.edu/~cjbuchan/Oddtown_and_Eventown_Module.pdf) for a combinatorial argument|
| 17 Oct, 2025 | _Nimbers – I_ <br /> See [this essay](https://jdh.hamkins.org/win-at-nim-the-secret-mathematical-strategy/) for a build up of the XOR strategy.|
| 20 Oct, 2025 | _Nimbers – II_ <br /> For further reading, [check out transfinite Nim](https://jdh.hamkins.org/transfinite-nim/).|
| 24 Oct, 2025 | _Counting Incomplete Cubes_ <br /> Phenomenal tour of the problem by Paul Duncstep [here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_BrFKp-U8GI).|
| 27 Oct, 2025 | _Group Actions with Tic-Tac-Toe_ <br /> Check out the [Numberphile take](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wdDF7_vfLcE) on this problem.|
| 28 Oct, 2025 | _Burnside with Colored Squares_ <br /> We analyzed the number of squares whose edges have been colored with three colors (where not every color has to necessarily appear) discounting symmetries. Find more practice problems in [this worksheet](https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/xywo3afkcesiq70y416xj/burnside.pdf?rlkey=evcxmoq6mva5zn3n9qt5me6dp&st=tamk9j8g&dl=0).|
| 31 Oct, 2025 | _Permutation Puzzles_ <br /> This material was inspired by [this course](https://www.sfu.ca/~jtmulhol/math302/). In particular, download the [course book](https://www.sfu.ca/~jtmulhol/math302/notes/permutation-puzzles-book.pdf) and look at section 5.2 for the questions discussed in class. <br /> General challenge: can you figure out how Penn and Teller were [fooled here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aZpA1k-sDcg)?|
| 03 Nov, 2025 | _Rubik Cube + Commutators_ <br /> This class was a poor imitation of Ramprasad's [amazing talk](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O5eH3x3sTNA) on the topic. You can find the cubing demos [here](https://www.tcs.tifr.res.in/~ramprasad/cubing/). The material on solving equations was out of scope for our class, but you are welcome to explore it as further reading!|
| 04 Nov, 2025 | _Commutators Continued_ <br /> In this class we went over the commutator theorem. |
| 10 Nov, 2025 | _Card Magic · Ternary and Binary_ <br /> For practice with ternary representations, see the [27 card trick](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=l7lP9y7Bb5g) · For an application of binary representations, check out [any card to any position](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8aHq_euaxPE) using only perfect in and out shuffles. |
| 11 Nov, 2025 | _Basic Number Theory_ <br /> We mostly focused on the Fundamental Theorem of Arithmetic. <br /> See the following essays by Gowers on the topic for additional reading: [Why isn’t the fundamental theorem of arithmetic obvious?](https://gowers.wordpress.com/2011/11/13/why-isnt-the-fundamental-theorem-of-arithmetic-obvious?) and [Proving the fundamental theorem of arithmetic](https://gowers.wordpress.com/2011/11/18/proving-the-fundamental-theorem-of-arithmetic/)|
| 14 Nov, 2025 | _Enigma_ <br /> See Rejewski's [1980 paper](https://cryptocellar.org/enigma/files/rew80.pdf) and [these notes](https://people.cs.uchicago.edu/~davidcash/284-autumn-19/02-permutations-and-enigma.pdf). |
| 17 Nov, 2025 | _Same-Size Intersections_ <br /> We did another example of applying the linear algebraic method. <br /> See the book [Thirty-three Miniatures: Mathematical and Algorithmic Applications of Linear Algebra](https://kam.mff.cuni.cz/~matousek/stml-53-matousek-1.pdf) by Matoušek for more examples. What we discussed in class was based on Miniature #4.|
