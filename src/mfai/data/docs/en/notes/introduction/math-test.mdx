# Mathematical Equations Test

This page demonstrates various mathematical notations using MathJax.

## Inline Math
$$
 \begin{equation*}
     |x| =
         \begin{cases}
               -x & \text{if } x < 0,\\
                     0 & \text{if } x = 0,\\
                           x & \text{if } x > 0\\
         \end{cases}
                               \end{equation*}
$$
Here's an inline equation: $E = mc^2$ represents Einstein's famous equation.

The quadratic formula is $x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$.

## Display Math

The normal distribution probability density function:

$$
f(x) = \frac{1}{\sigma\sqrt{2\pi}} e^{-\frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma}\right)^2}
$$

## Matrix Example

A simple matrix multiplication:

$$
\begin{bmatrix}
a & b \\
c & d
\end{bmatrix}
\begin{bmatrix}
x \\
y
\end{bmatrix}
=
\begin{bmatrix}
ax + by \\
cx + dy
\end{bmatrix}
$$

## Calculus

The fundamental theorem of calculus:

$$
\int_a^b f(x)\,dx = F(b) - F(a)
$$

Where $F'(x) = f(x)$.

## Summation and Products

Sum notation:
$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n} i = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}
$$

Product notation:
$$
\prod_{i=1}^{n} x_i = x_1 \cdot x_2 \cdot \ldots \cdot x_n
$$

## Complex Equations

The Schrödinger equation:

$$
i\hbar\frac{\partial}{\partial t}\Psi(x,t) = \hat{H}\Psi(x,t)
$$

## Set Theory

$$
A \cup B = \{x : x \in A \text{ or } x \in B\}
$$

$$
A \cap B = \{x : x \in A \text{ and } x \in B\}
$$

## Greek Letters

- Alpha: $\alpha$, Beta: $\beta$, Gamma: $\gamma$
- Delta: $\Delta$, Theta: $\theta$, Lambda: $\lambda$
- Sigma: $\Sigma$, Pi: $\Pi$, Omega: $\Omega$

## Limits

$$
\lim_{x \to \infty} \left(1 + \frac{1}{x}\right)^x = e
$$

## Vectors

Vector notation: $\vec{v} = \langle v_1, v_2, v_3 \rangle$

Dot product: $\vec{a} \cdot \vec{b} = |\vec{a}||\vec{b}|\cos\theta$
